Method

The English text is a reading reference. All official language versions have equal authority. Use Language to see only its published differences and English-text highlights. Select highlighted text to see original wording, an English translation, and the difference in meaning.

Impact narrows the findings and highlights to an assigned impact level. It can be combined with Language. No impact rating yet means no level has been assigned for that finding; an issue explanation may be under review. It does not mean Limited impact. Earlier assessments await reassessment on the Major/Limited scale. The focus is kept in shared links.

Published findings are supported by independent model analyses and evidence review. They identify differences in meaning. Findings are provisional and do not establish that a language version is legally wrong. An act with no findings is not necessarily equivalent across languages; check its review coverage and source version.

One comparison unit means one English passage checked against one other language.

Each language-specific finding counts as one difference in meaning. Findings quoting the same English wording in the same passage appear together in a comparison group, which can contain different legal issues. Both filter menus count findings matching the other filter; the list and navigation show comparison groups.

English translations of other language versions are AI-generated reading aids. Underlining marks wording differences between the two English quotations; the explanations describe their significance.

Potential impact describes how much the legal outcome could change if an interpretation were accepted. Issues needing more context have no rating. They are grouped by legal issue, which may span several highlighted passages. A shared English passage may contain several distinct issues. An act without impact proposals is not assessed as having no impact.

Assigned ratings marked provisional are AI assessments. Confidence describes support for the proposed legal effect, separately from impact size. Review completion applies to this text version and available source scope.

The potential impact scale applies to the possible legal outcome:

  • Major: A material change to an important right, core duty or permission, eligibility, remedy, substantial financial liability or sanction.
  • Limited: A narrower or ancillary legal consequence, with no supported major consequence.
