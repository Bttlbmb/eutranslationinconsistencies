Method

The English text is a reading reference. All official language versions have equal authority. Select highlighted text to see original wording, an English translation, and the potential difference.

Published findings are supported by independent model analyses and evidence review. They identify possible differences in meaning, not adjudicated errors or legal advice. An act with no findings is not necessarily equivalent across languages; check its review coverage and source version.
