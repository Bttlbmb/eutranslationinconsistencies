Method

All official language versions have equal authority. English is shown only as a common reading reference.

Reading a finding

  • Select highlighted text to see the other language’s official wording, an AI translation into English and what differs in meaning. Shading marks the words that differ between the official English and the translation.
  • Language and Impact narrow the list and the highlights. Shared links keep both.

What a finding is

  • A difference in meaning found by AI review and checked against the exact source wording. Findings are provisional and do not establish that a language version is legally wrong.
  • Each language-specific finding counts once. Findings quoting the same English wording in the same passage are shown together.
  • Coverage counts each English passage checked against each other language, for the recorded text version. No findings does not prove the versions are equivalent; check coverage and the source version.

Potential impact

How much the legal outcome could change if the differing reading were accepted. One issue can link several findings. Ratings are provisional AI assessments; confidence in the proposed legal effect is shown separately.

Major
A material change to an important right, core duty or permission, eligibility, remedy, substantial financial liability or sanction.
Limited
A narrower or ancillary legal consequence, with no supported major consequence.
No impact rating yet
Not yet assessed, under review, or an earlier assessment awaiting the Major/Limited scale. It does not mean Limited.
