Method

All official language versions have equal authority. English provides a common reading reference.

Published findings describe possible differences in meaning supported by two independent AI scans and a separate evidence review. They are not adjudicated legal errors.

Language findings are published finding records. The reader combines records with the same English quotation in the same passage into grouped comparisons. A group may contain several distinct issues. Expand a language list to see its count and open the focused reader.

One comparison means one English passage checked against one other language. Coverage refers to the recorded source version. No published findings can reflect an unfinished review; a completed review does not establish equivalence of every legal reading.

“Substantial” is the publication threshold for findings. The separate potential-impact bar shows High potential impact, Moderate, or Limited estimates when a level has been assigned. A single legal issue may span several finding records. “No impact rating yet” means no level has been assigned; an issue explanation may still be under review. It does not mean Limited impact. Ratings describe a possible change in legal outcome if a reading prevails, with confidence and uncertainty available in the reader.
