ABOUT CONCORDANCE

One law.
Many language versions.

Concordance helps you explore potential differences in legal meaning between official EU language versions. English provides a shared starting point for reading; it has no higher authority than the other official versions.

  1. Read the source.The English text sits alongside the exact version and links to the official EUR-Lex record.
  2. Follow a highlight.Gold passages open original-language evidence, an English translation, and a concise account of the potential difference.
  3. Examine the finding.Two independent model analyses are cross-checked by a reviewer. Only supported findings are published. Each finding shows its review details.
A starting point for scrutiny

Findings are machine-assisted research, not adjudicated errors or legal advice. An analysis with no findings does not establish that every version is equivalent. Coverage and completion status are shown for each act.

This is a read-only snapshot. The owner imports and analyzes legislation locally; no submissions or analysis run in your browser.
