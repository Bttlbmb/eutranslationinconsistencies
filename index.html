Method

All official language versions have equal authority. English provides a common reading reference.

Published findings describe differences in meaning supported by two independent AI scans and a separate evidence review. A finding does not establish that a language version is legally wrong.

Each language-specific finding counts once. Findings quoting the same English wording in the same passage are displayed together in a comparison group; a group can contain different legal issues. Expand a language list to see each language’s count.

One comparison means one English passage checked against one other language. Coverage refers to the recorded source version. No published findings can reflect an unfinished review; a completed review does not establish equivalence of every legal reading.

Potential impact describes how much the legal outcome could change if an interpretation were accepted. High potential impact, Moderate and Limited are assigned ratings. A single legal issue may link several findings. Ratings and assessment progress are shown separately: an assessment can remain under review without a rating. Provisional AI assessments and confidence in the proposed legal effect are identified in the reader.
